Getting Started

CobbleClub is a Fabric 1.21.1 Cobblemon server with custom progression, worlds, economy, claims, kits, crates and cosmetics.

First Join

New players receive the Newb kit automatically. It is designed to provide the essential tools needed to begin exploring and building.

Starter Pokémon: Follow the server's starter prompt when you first join.

Where to go next

Use the server's wild-world system to leave the protected hub/spawn area and begin exploring. CobbleClub has four custom wild worlds: Red, Blue, Green and Yellow.

Protecting a home

Use your CobbleClub claim tool and claim blocks to protect the area where you build. See the Claims page before expanding a large base.

Progression

Playing, catching Pokémon, voting, kits, ranks, crates and server activities all feed into CobbleClub's progression systems.
