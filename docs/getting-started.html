Your first 30 minutes

A simple order of operations for a brand-new CobbleClub player. You do not need to memorize every system before you start.

First login

1. Join and open your dashboard

1

Open `/club`

This is the main CobbleClub dashboard. It shows your PokéDollars, Gems, claim capacity, daily status and crate keys, and gives quick access to several custom menus.

2

Know your starting balances

A new player starts with 400 PokéDollars, 5 Gems and 700 claim blocks.

3

Keep the Newb Kit tools

The Newb Kit includes your CobbleClub Claiming Tool, a diamond pickaxe, Pokédex, Poké Balls, food, PC and Healing Machine.

Starter & Pokémon

2. Pick a Pokémon and learn the core Cobblemon loop

Core loop

  • Explore until you find Pokémon you want.
  • Battle and catch them as normal through Cobblemon.
  • Your captures can earn CobbleClub PokéDollars.
  • Use your Pokédex and player-facing scanner/indicator mods to track progress.

Useful references

When you need species mechanics, evolution methods, items or Cobblemon blocks, use the official Cobblemon wiki from our Modpack References page.

Wild Worlds

3. Go somewhere meant for exploration

1

Type `/wild`

Choose any of the nine managed worlds from the custom 3×3 menu.

2

Or use `/rtp`

Random teleport chooses from the colored adventure worlds. You can also target one, for example `/rtp purple` or `/rtp resource`.

3

Stand still for the warmup

RTP has a 3-second warmup and cancels if you move. A successful RTP has a 5-minute cooldown.

Current command
The Resource World is for renewable gathering. The old `/resourceworld tp resource` instruction is obsolete on the current CobbleClub build—use `/wild` or `/rtp resource` instead.
Build a home

4. Protect a place before you invest in it

1

Type `/claim`

Open the map. Click a chunk, or Shift-drag across chunks, to begin a selection.

2

Confirm the selection

A Minecraft chunk consumes 256 claim blocks. Your starting 700 blocks is enough for a small protected base.

3

Configure access

Select the claim to manage trust, permissions, bans, messages, subclaims, ownership and safe teleporting.

4

Expand later

Buy +256 claim blocks from the CobbleClub interface with Gems. The first purchase costs 5 Gems and the price scales after each successful purchase.

Where to go next

6. Pick a long-term goal

Builder

Build & trade

Expand your claim, create a `/chestshop`, build storage and participate in the player economy.

Collector

Collect Pokémon

Work on Pokédex progress, shinies, legendaries, special forms and custom Pokémon previews.

Battler

Battle

Use the trainer, raid, Mega Showdown and battle-enhancement mods included in the pack.

Style

Cosmetics

Earn or buy wardrobe items, glows and tags; save multiple wardrobe presets.
