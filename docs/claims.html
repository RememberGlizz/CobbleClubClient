Claims

CobbleClub's custom claim system lets players protect their builds and control access to claimed land.

Starting claim blocks

Players begin with 700 claim blocks. Additional claim capacity is part of the server's progression system.

Claim Tool

The CobbleClub claim tool is included in the Newb kit and is used to interact with the claiming system.

Permissions

Claims are intended to give the owner control over actions within their protected area. Administrative claim controls are kept separate from normal player permissions.

Tip: Claim important builds before storing valuable items or investing heavily in a base.
