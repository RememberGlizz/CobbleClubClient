Frequently Asked Questions

What version is CobbleClub?

The current server/mod environment is based on Minecraft Java Edition 1.21.1 using Fabric and Cobblemon.

Where do I explore?

CobbleClub has Red, Blue, Green and Yellow wild worlds, plus a separate resource world.

How do I protect my base?

Use the custom claim system and the claim tool provided to new players.

What currencies are used?

PokéDollars are the main economy currency and Gems are a separate progression currency.

Are cosmetics pay-to-win?

The wardrobe's hats, wings, floaties, balloons and glow effects are visual customization features.

Where can I see all commands?

See the Commands page.
