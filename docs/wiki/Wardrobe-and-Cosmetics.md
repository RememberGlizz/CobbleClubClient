# Wardrobe & Cosmetics

## Categories

- Hats
- Wings
- Floaties
- Balloons
- Glow

## Rendering

CobbleClub uses custom client rendering for cosmetics.

Important design goals:

- Proper 3D positioning
- Correct player-model attachment
- No flat paper-like helmet replacements
- Stable orientation
- No oversized glow boxes
- Consistent preview behavior

## Known Design Rules

Working cosmetics should not be globally altered while fixing unrelated cosmetics.

Known-good wing/model alignment should remain isolated from hat/floatie/balloon fixes.
