Crates

CobbleClub has three custom crate tiers with different themes and reward pools.

Vote Crate

Earned through voting. Includes normal Pokémon dolls among its exclusive collectible rewards.

Shiny Crate

Shiny-themed rewards and shiny Pokémon dolls.

Legendary Crate

High-tier rewards and Legendary Pokémon dolls.

Keys

Each crate uses its matching key type. Keys are integrated with CobbleClub's server systems so balances remain synchronized.

Vote Crate: Unlike the paid crate tiers, the Vote crate is intended to be earned by voting rather than purchased with Gems.
