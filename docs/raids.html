Pokémon progression

Raids, battles & Pokémon add-ons

CobbleClub combines its own progression systems with a large set of Cobblemon add-ons. This page explains the main gameplay buckets; the external reference page links the official documentation for each mod.

Group / boss content

Raid Dens

Cobblemon Raid Dens adds raid-focused encounters to the pack. Exact raid mechanics, drops and compatibility change with the add-on version, so use the official Raid Dens reference linked on the Modpack References page for mechanics rather than relying on an old server-guide copy.

Battle gimmicks

Mega Showdown

Mega Showdown is part of the pack and supplies extended battle mechanics/content used by other add-ons, including systems that rely on Mega/other gimmick support. Its dedicated wiki is linked in the reference hub.

RCT + More Radical Trainers

Trainer progression

Radical Cobblemon Trainers provides large numbers of structured NPC trainer encounters and progression. More Radical Trainers adds additional series/content on top of it. Their own documentation is the correct source for trainer-series prerequisites, badges and level-cap mechanics.

Legendary Monuments

Legendaries

Legendary Monuments adds structured ways to obtain many Legendary/Mythical Pokémon through unique mechanics, structures and items instead of only waiting for ordinary spawns. Because individual legendary methods are extensive, use its full external wiki from our reference hub.
