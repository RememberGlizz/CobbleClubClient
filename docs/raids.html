Raids

CobbleClub includes Cobblemon raid content, including a dedicated raid dimension supplied by the server's raid system.

Raid Pokémon

Pokémon used by an active raid can be created and discarded as part of the raid lifecycle. These temporary raid entities are separate from a player's normal party Pokémon.
