Player economy

Chest Shops

Chest Shops turn a chest inside your CobbleClub claim into a protected player shop. The server handles price, stock, item transfer and PokéDollar payment.

Seller setup

Create a shop

1

Place a chest inside your claim

The chest must be in CobbleClub-protected land where your role has the Chest Shops permission. By default, shop creation is Owner-only.

2

Run `/chestshop`

This arms shop-placement mode. The instruction appears on screen; it is intentionally not duplicated into chat.

3

Right-click a side of the chest

Click a horizontal side—not the top or bottom. CobbleClub needs room for the shop sign.

4

Choose the item

The item-selection screen opens. Choose a non-empty inventory item that this chest will sell.

5

Set the unit price

The price screen shows server guidance/minimums based on the economy. Confirm your desired unit price.

6

Stock the chest

Put the sell item into the chest. The sign/shop state updates to reflect item, price and stock.

Manage

Seller controls

Normal right-click

As the shop owner/manager, normal interaction with the chest is used for stocking and inventory access.

Sneak-click / shop sign

Use the management interaction to open shop controls rather than buying from your own listing.

/chestshop cancel

Cancel placement mode if you changed your mind before creating the shop.

Buyer flow

What happens when someone buys

1

Buyer opens the shop

The shop UI shows the item, unit price and available stock.

2

Server re-checks everything

CobbleClub verifies balance and stock on the server instead of trusting the client screen.

3

Money and item move together

The buyer is charged, the item is transferred, and PokéDollars are deposited directly to the shop owner.

4

Failed stock changes are protected

If the stock changed unexpectedly during the purchase, the transaction logic can refund rather than silently eating money.

Designed for public shops
Claim management permission and shopping access are separate concepts. Visitors can normally buy from a shop even though they cannot create or manage that shop, unless the claim's other access rules block them.
Practical tips

Good shop setup

Tip

Make a market subclaim

Use a dedicated subclaim with controlled permissions so customers can enter/use the shop area without gaining build access.

Tip

Keep stock simple

One shop sells one configured item. Use multiple chests for a clean marketplace.

Tip

Price from `/sell`

The server gives price guidance. Player shops can charge more than server sell value when scarcity/effort justifies it.

Tip

Use claim messages

Entry messages can identify your market, rules or shop owner.

Tip

Check access

If buyers cannot interact, review claim role permissions/bans before rebuilding the shop.

Tip

Use `/pay` for negotiated trades

For one-off deals, direct payments are simpler than creating a permanent shop.
