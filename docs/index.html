Your first five commands

/club

Open the main CobbleClub dashboard for your money, Gems, claims, kits, tags, wardrobe and crate previews.

/wild

Open the custom nine-world selector and choose where you want to explore.

/claim

Open the claim map and protect your base.

/sell

Open the sell menu and turn supported resources into PokéDollars.

/wiki

Get back to this wiki from inside the server.

/features

CobbleClub custom systems

Built around progression, not random commands

Custom

Custom Dashboard

Use `/club` as your home base for CobbleClub systems, balances and progression.

Custom

Protected Player Claims

Map-based claims, trust roles, permissions, subclaims, safe teleporting and scalable claim-block progression.

Custom

Player Chest Shops

Turn a chest inside your claim into a secure shop with server-authoritative payments and stock.

Custom

Nine Wild Worlds

Choose a colored Wild World or Resource World, or use safe random teleport with warmup and cooldown.

Custom

Economy & Contracts

Earn PokéDollars from playtime, catches, battles, contracts, selling and voting; spend Gems on claim capacity.

Custom

Kits & Rank Progression

Exact Newb, Ace, Champion, Master and Legend kits with controlled cooldown reductions.

Custom

Crates & Key Wallet

Vote, Shiny and Legendary keys all use the same CobbleClub key wallet and unlock distinct reward pools.

Custom

Wardrobe & Cosmetics

Equip hats, wings, floaties, balloons and glow effects, save presets and purchase selected cosmetics.

Custom

Graphical Tags

Rank badges, Trainer, staff tags and the purchasable Shiny Hunter tag rendered with CobbleClub's custom tag font.

Support the network

Vote for CobbleClub

Voting supports the server and, when a vote reaches the server through the voting system, the default CobbleClub reward is 250 PokéDollars + 1 Vote Key.
