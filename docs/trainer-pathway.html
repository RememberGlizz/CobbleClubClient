How gyms work

Trainer paths, not single boss fights

Each Kanto gym has themed prerequisite trainer battles before the leader. RCT handles the battle teams, AI, progression requirements and level-cap flow. CobbleClub adds the server-specific reward and announcement layer around those wins.

RCT

Progression gates

Required trainers lead into the gym leader instead of letting players skip straight to every badge.

Levels

Natural cap growth

The next required trainer drives the practical level cap so the player grows with the route.

Difficulty

Gradual AI ramp

Early trainers are forgiving. Switching, status, recovery and item use become more important later.

Rewards

Badges and first-clear rewards

Gym leaders award the correct Kanto badge. CobbleverseBadges is the preferred item source, with compatible fallback handling for CobblemonPokemonBadges-style items.

First-clear rewards

  • PokéDollars
  • Gems
  • Rare Candy
  • Correct gym badge
  • League/Champion bonuses
  • Kanto League Trophy at Champion completion

Safe delivery

If the player's inventory is full, the reward drops directly in front of them. Only that player can pick it up for the first 20 seconds, and they receive an inventory-full message so the reward is not forgotten.

Server moments

Major victory announcements

Major progression wins can announce the player, the opponent they defeated and the Pokémon party they used. Gym clears can also announce the badge earned, while Champion completion receives a Hall of Fame-style message.

Where this is going

Kanto is only the first quarter

Important balancing rule
Giovanni is the end of Kanto, not the end of CobbleClub's trainer progression. Johto should start approachable for a Kanto graduate, then Hoenn and Sinnoh continue raising the overall ceiling. The full difficulty curve is designed across all 32 badges.

The long-term goal is for every region to bring its own appropriate Pokémon pool, trainer themes, gym leaders, canonical badges and league progression so the journey feels like a connected Pokémon series inside Minecraft.
