What the menu can do

Useful controls

  • Equip / unequip owned cosmetics.
  • Unequip All clears your equipped cosmetics and active glow.
  • Show / hide lets you keep a setup saved while controlling whether wardrobe visuals are rendered.
  • Color controls appear on dyeable items. Some cosmetics, such as the Eevee Explorer Hood, intentionally do not support recoloring.
  • Presets 1–5 let you save complete looks and switch back later.
  • Direct purchase is available for configured locked cosmetics/glows that have a PokéDollar price.
