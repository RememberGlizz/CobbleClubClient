Wardrobe & Cosmetics

The CobbleClub wardrobe provides cosmetic customization without replacing the server's core Pokémon gameplay.

Hats

3D head cosmetics and Pokémon-inspired designs.

Wings

Back cosmetics based around CobbleClub's fitted wing model.

Floaties

Wearable body cosmetics.

Balloons

Pokémon-themed balloon cosmetics.

Glow

Player glow effects with selectable colors where supported.

Equipping

Open the CobbleClub wardrobe interface and choose a cosmetic category. Cosmetics are designed to remain visual rather than provide combat advantages.
