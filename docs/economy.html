Selling items

1

Run `/sell`

The custom sell screen shows supported items in your inventory and their current values.

2

Choose what to sell

Use the GUI, or use `/sell hand` for the held stack and `/sell all` for supported inventory items.

3

Check values first

`/sell prices` shows current pricing. The server uses a curated economy table rather than paying for every random modded item.

4

Know the PokéBlocks rule

Pokémon dolls are recognized and priced by rarity/variant, but utility currencies, passes, vouchers and similar items are deliberately excluded to prevent economy exploits.

Progression currency

Gems and claim expansion

Gems are not the same as PokéDollars. You begin with 5 Gems and earn 2 Gems every 30 minutes of playtime by default.

Claim purchase

Each purchase adds 256 claim blocks. The first purchase costs 5 Gems; after each successful purchase the price is multiplied by 2.7×.

Price keeps its history

Your purchase count persists through relogs, restarts and deleting claims. Deleting land frees used claim blocks, but it does not rewind your expansion-price progression.

Scaling cost
Approximate early prices: 5 → 14 → 36 → 98 → 266 → 717 → 1,937 → 5,230 Gems. The server calculates the authoritative current price and shows it in the CobbleClub UI.
Player payments

Sending money

/pay <player> <amount>

Transfers PokéDollars from your balance to another player. Use this for direct trades when you are not using a Chest Shop.

/baltop

View the PokéDollar leaderboard.

Death penalty
Death removes 100 PokéDollars by default. Keep this in mind before carrying out risky exploration or battles.
