Economy & Progression

CobbleClub uses PokéDollars and Gems as its main custom progression currencies. They are deliberately earned in different ways and are used by different server systems.

Death

The configured CobbleClub economy includes a 100 PokéDollar death penalty. Keep this in mind when carrying out dangerous exploration or other high-risk activities.

Checking your balance

/bal

This is the primary confirmed player-facing balance command.

Gems and claims

New players begin with 700 claim blocks. Gems are part of the progression path for acquiring additional claim capacity, allowing active players to protect increasingly large builds over time.

Kits and cooldown reduction

Rank kits operate on cooldowns. CobbleClub's kit system includes an economy-based cooldown-reduction mechanic with limits, rather than allowing unlimited kit claims. See the Kits & Ranks page for the actual kit contents.

Crates and voting

Vote, Shiny and Legendary crates form another reward path. Vote rewards are intentionally earned rather than bought through the same Gems purchase option used by the premium crate types. Crates can contain useful gameplay rewards and collectibles.

How should a new player use money?

Early on, prioritize things that improve your ability to explore, catch Pokémon and establish a safe base. As your account becomes established, currency can go toward convenience, collection goals, cosmetics and other progression systems instead of basic survival.

Important: CobbleClub is designed so ordinary play itself progresses your account. You do not need to spend every session grinding one activity.
