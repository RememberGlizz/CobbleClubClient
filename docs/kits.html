Rank progression

Kits & ranks

Each premium rank has its own exact kit instead of automatically inheriting every lower kit. Newb is the default starter kit; Ace through Legend require the matching rank.

How it works

Kit access

Open /kits to see your eligible kit, cooldown and cooldown-reduction controls. New players receive the Newb Kit on first join; it can later be claimed again when its 12-hour cooldown allows.

No accidental kit stacking
Rank kits use an exact-rank gate. For example, Champion claims the Champion kit rather than stacking Ace + Champion simply because permissions inherit.

PokéDollar option

Reduce the current cooldown

The kit menu includes a −1h Cooldown purchase. Each one-hour reduction costs 500 PokéDollars by default.

Standard ranks

Up to 4 hours of reduction during a kit cycle.

Legend

Up to 6 hours of reduction during a kit cycle.

Per-cycle reduction
Cooldown reduction spends money; it does not permanently change the kit's base cooldown. Think of it as buying time off the current cycle.
