Kits & Ranks

CobbleClub has a starter kit plus rank-based kits with increasingly valuable rewards.

KitCooldownHighlights
NewbStarter / 12h windowClaim tool, diamond pickaxe, Pokédex, Poké Balls, food, PC and healing machine
Ace18hShiny Keys, Rare Candy, Ultra/Quick Balls and XP
Champion18hLegendary + Shiny Keys, Rare Candy, balls and XP
Master18hBeast Balls, Legendary/Shiny/Vote Keys and larger rewards
Legend18hMaster Ball, Shiny/Vote Keys and the largest candy/ball/XP bundle

Rank access

Rank kits require the corresponding server rank. Newb is the general starter kit.

Cooldown reduction

The server supports purchasing limited kit cooldown reductions through the economy, with rank-dependent limits.
