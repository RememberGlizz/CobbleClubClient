Player reference

Commands

Only normal player-facing commands are listed here. Staff administration commands are intentionally excluded.

Commands

Core

/club

Open the CobbleClub dashboard. `/cobbleclub` is an alias.

/features

Show a short in-game summary of server features.

/wiki

Get the CobbleClub wiki link.

/daily

Claim the daily reward when your 12-hour cooldown is ready.

Commands

Economy

/bal

Show your PokéDollar balance. `/balance` is the long form.

/baltop

Show the money leaderboard.

/pay <player> <amount>

Send PokéDollars to another online player.

/sell

Open the sell GUI.

/sell hand

Sell the supported item stack in your hand.

/sell all

Sell supported items from your inventory.

/sell prices

Show current sell pricing.

/contracts

Show your current rotating contracts. `/contract` is an alias.

Commands

Claims & shops

/claim

Open the claims map/menu. `/claims` and `/claim menu` also open it.

/claim tool

Recover a CobbleClub Claiming Tool if you do not currently have one.

/claim blocks

Show available and total claim blocks.

/chestshop

Arm Chest Shop placement mode, then right-click the side of a chest inside your claim.

/chestshop cancel

Leave Chest Shop placement mode without creating a shop.

Commands

Travel

/wild

Open the nine-world Wild selector.

/rtp

Random teleport into a colored Wild World.

/rtp <world>

Target `purple`, `red`, `orange`, `yellow`, `resource`, `blue`, `pink`, `green` or `cyan`.

/tpa <player>

Ask to teleport to another player.

/tpayes

Accept the current TPA request.

/tpano

Deny the current TPA request.

Commands

Kits, crates & customization

/kits

Open your kit menu. `/kit` is an alias.

/keys

Show your Vote, Shiny and Legendary key balances. `/crates` also shows them.

/crate preview <crate>

Preview a configured crate.

/crate open <crate>

Open a crate if you have the required key.

/tags

Open the tags menu. `/tag` is an alias.

/wardrobe

Open the CobbleClub wardrobe.

/pokemonpreview

Open the Pokémon Styles preview/catalog.

/gearpreview

Open the gear preview.

/cratepreview <crate>

Open a crate preview menu.

Third-party commands
You may see additional commands from third-party mods in the pack. Their official project/wikis are linked on the Modpack References page.
