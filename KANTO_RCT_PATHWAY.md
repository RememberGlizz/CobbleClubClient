# CobbleClub Kanto RCT Pathway

This pathway ships as mod data inside CobbleClub and is consumed by Radical Cobblemon Trainers.

Progression:
Pewter → Cerulean → Vermilion → Celadon → Fuchsia → Saffron → Cinnabar → Viridian → Lorelei → Bruno → Agatha → Lance → Champion.

Each gym requires two themed CobbleClub gym trainers before its leader. Gym trainers require the previous badge. Leaders and League members allow unlimited retries after losses but only count one progression defeat per player.

Difficulty philosophy:
- Early gyms: forgiving, small teams, imperfect AI.
- Midgame: status, recovery and switching become relevant.
- Sabrina/Blaine/Giovanni: tactical without competitive-level punishment.
- Elite Four and Champion: strong full teams, smarter switching and up to two item uses.

First-defeat milestones are wired through RCT's defeat-count advancement trigger into CobbleClub's reward service.
